Reference photographs used to generate the offline stock card previews:

- `portrait.jpg`: "Woman smiling in front of graffiti (Unsplash)" by Paulette Wooten, CC0 1.0. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Woman_smiling_in_front_of_graffiti_(Unsplash).jpg
- `landscape.jpg`: "Mountain Lake Sunset" by 420 Photography, CC0 1.0. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mountain_Lake_Sunset.jpg
- `night.jpg`: "Lights in Motion (Unsplash)" by Alejandro Benėt, CC0 1.0. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Lights_in_Motion_(Unsplash).jpg

Each card is generated from one of these three photographs through the bundled SpektraLab engine at its default exposure. Film cards use the stock's recommended print material, or a direct scan for positive film. Paper cards use a fixed compatible reference film: Portra 400 for color paper, Vision3 250D for cine print stock, and Tri-X 400 for black-and-white paper. These are examples; results on a user's photograph depend on the image and settings.
